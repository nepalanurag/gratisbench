What these tools do

The audio tools cover the small jobs: Audio trimmer cuts a section out of a recording with a waveform editor, Audio merger joins several clips with smooth fades at each join, and Audio converter moves files between MP3, WAV, and OGG. The video tools handle the heavy jobs with a real ffmpeg encoder running in your browser: Video compressor shrinks a file to the size a chat app or email will accept, Video trimmer cuts out a section, and Video converter changes containers and codecs. Screen recorder captures a tab, window, or screen with no time cap.

A useful pattern: record a demo with the screen recorder, trim the dead air off the start with the video trimmer, then compress the result to 25MB so it sends in a chat message. Or trim a voice memo, join several takes with the merger, and convert the result to MP3 for sharing. Everything stays on your device the whole way through.

Common questions

Are these media tools free?
Yes. All seven tools are free with no limits, no watermarks, and no account. The video tools download a roughly 30MB encoder on first use, which your browser caches; the tools themselves cost nothing.
Do my files get uploaded?
No. All seven tools process your files locally in the browser. Nothing is sent to a server, which is why they also work offline once loaded. The honest exception is the first load of the video encoder, which downloads code, not your files.
Why is video encoding slow?
Video encoding in a browser runs without the hardware acceleration that desktop apps use, so it is roughly five to fifty times slower than native software. Short clips take minutes; long 4K files can take a very long time. The tools show progress bars and ask you to keep the tab open.
Will my files lose quality?
It depends on the job. Trimming and converting audio to WAV are lossless. Compressing video, converting video, and encoding MP3 all trade some quality for size; the tools use high-quality settings and say plainly where the tradeoff is. Fast video trim copies the stream with zero quality change.
