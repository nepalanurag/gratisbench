Common questions

Is the device mockup generator really free?
Yes. There is no account, no watermark, and no limit on how many mockups you make. The frame is drawn on a canvas in your browser, so there is no server cost to recover. Placeit charges $14.95 a month for a stock-photo library of devices in hands; if you want a clean geometric frame around your screenshot, this does it for nothing.
What frames are available?
Three: a browser window with traffic-light dots and a URL bar, a phone with a notch and side buttons, and a laptop with a screen bezel and a deck. The frames are drawn fresh from your settings every time, not stock photos, so they stay crisp at any export size.
What image can I upload?
Any PNG, JPG, or WebP screenshot. The image is fitted inside the frame’s screen area automatically (contain-fit), so it does not matter if your screenshot is portrait, landscape, or an odd size. Very large screenshots work too, though the export tops out at 1600 pixels wide.
Does the transparent background really export transparent?
Yes. The checkerboard you see in the preview is only a preview hint drawn with CSS; the exported PNG keeps real alpha transparency, so the mockup composites cleanly over any background you place it on. Most free tools bake the checkerboard into the file.
Is my screenshot private?
Yes. The image is read by your browser, drawn onto a canvas, and downloaded from there. It is never uploaded to any server, because there is no server involved. For unreleased products and client work, that is a meaningful difference from tools that render on someone else’s machine.
Can I change the URL shown in the browser frame?
Not in v1. The browser frame shows a placeholder address. The frame is about presenting the screenshot, not faking a live site. If the URL matters, take the screenshot with it visible.
Do I need an account?
No, and there is no way to create one. Your frame, background, padding, and shadow settings are saved in this browser automatically so your next mockup starts where the last one left off. The screenshot itself is never stored; upload it again each visit.
