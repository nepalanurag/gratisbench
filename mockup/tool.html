How it works +
  1. Drop two or more PDFs into the box above.
  2. Drag rows to set the page order.
  3. Hit Merge PDFs and download the combined file.
Everything happens in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, so there's no waiting on a server and no file-size queue.

Common questions

Is there a page limit?
No hard limit. Very large files (hundreds of pages) may take a few seconds on slower devices since all processing is local.
Are my documents private?
Yes. Files are read and merged inside your browser tab and never sent anywhere.
Can I reorder pages?
Drag any row up or down before merging to change the order.
